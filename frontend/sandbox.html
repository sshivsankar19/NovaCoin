What is happening?

  • Transaction: waits to be included in a block.
  • Mempool: holds pending simulated transactions.
  • Proof of Work: the nonce changes until the hash meets the target.
  • Difficulty: 3 means the hash begins with three zero characters.
  • Reward: confirmation adds 10 NVC after the transaction amount is accounted for.
